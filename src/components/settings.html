Radar

Reflectivity gates ("pixels") with dBz values below this threshold will be hidden. Turn up to increase performance, or down to view more radar clutter.

Palettes are .pal files that tell the radar how to color the radar mesh.

Keyboard Shortcuts

In the future, these will be customizable.

R

Press this key to show the radar status dialog, which includes information about the current radar station and radar product.

H

Press this key to open the main menu, where you can change the radar station, toggle radar layers, and more.

L

Press this key to open the layer menu, where you can toggle individual radar layers on and off.
