General Settings

Experience

When an item is clicked from a popup, choose whether it appears in a movable, resizable window or a fixed dialog.

If enabled, right-click (or press and hold) on the map to access a location menu.

In dual map mode, show a dot on the opposite map of the mouse cursor, at the same position as the mouse cursor.

Location

Enable to use location-based features such as Spotter Network location reporting and to view your location on the map.

Debugging

Turn notifications for internal errors on or off. Some errors you see may not actually be issues.

Settings management

Enter an existing or new passphrase. Enter the same passphrase on other devices to sync your settings. Anyone with your passphrase can update your settings. This will not sync your Spotter Network login or color palettes. Stored settings will be deleted from the server after 2 months of inactivity.

Click to export all settings to a JSON file. This does not back up your Spotter Network login or color palettes.

Click to import settings from an exported JSON file.

Click to reset all settings to their defaults. This will not sign you out of Spotter Network.

Radar

Customize the appearance and behavior of the radar layer.

General

Reflectivity gates ("pixels") with dBz values below this threshold will be hidden. Turn up to increase performance, or down to view more radar clutter.

Enable dealiasing for Level-II velocity. Dealiasing attempts to correct incorrect velocity gates by guessing their correct values based on the surrounding gates. May increase loading times by about a second.

Animation

Animate this many frames in the animate tool. The maximum number of frames is limited to the cache slot limit below. SparkRadar recommends 6-12 frames.

Adjust the delay in seconds between frames in the animate tool. The smaller the value, the faster the animation.

Customization

Palettes are .pal files that tell the radar how to color the radar mesh. You can create your own palettes or download some here.

Radar Cache ADVANCED

Maximum number of radar layers to keep in memory. Any given radar layer is defined by a radial scan at a given station, tilt, time, and product. Set to 1 to essentially disable caching.

Use up to this much memory for radar cache. Set to 0 to disable and only use cache slot limits.
